## Amount / mechanics quick reference

- **Bird (QVFA, EXC-002):** up to player max; raises per §5(a)(2).
- **Non-Bird (EXC-003):** greater of 120% prior final-year salary+bonuses, 120% of min, or own-RFA qualifying offer; raises per §5(a)(1).
- **Early Bird (EXC-004):** ≥2-season term; greater of 175% prior salary+bonuses or 105% prior-year Average Player Salary; raises per §5(a)(2).
- **Disabled Player (EXC-005):** sign — lesser of 50% of disabled player's salary or the Non-Taxpayer MLE; acquire — that lesser amount + $100K; 1-season term; apply Jul 1–Jan 15; expires Mar 10.
- **Bi-annual (EXC-011):** 3.32% of cap; max 2 seasons; not in consecutive years; blocked by Room MLE use.
- **Non-Taxpayer MLE (EXC-012):** 9.12% of cap; max 4 seasons.
- **Taxpayer MLE (EXC-013):** $5M (2023-24), indexed to cap thereafter; max 2 seasons; requires Apron Team Salary above First Apron Level after use.
- **Room MLE (EXC-014):** 5.678% of cap; max 3 seasons; once used, bars Bi-annual/Non-Taxpayer/Taxpayer MLE that year.
- **Minimum (EXC-016):** Minimum Player Salary, no bonuses, max 2 seasons.
- **Traded Player matching (EXC-017–021):** Standard 100%+$250K; Aggregated Standard 100% combined+$250K; Transition (2023-24) 110%+$250K; Expanded greater-of formula; Room 100% cap room+$250K. The $250K allowance drops to $0 if post-trade Apron Team Salary exceeds the First Apron Level (§6(j)(3)).
- **Second Round Pick (EXC-025):** 2+1 or 3+1 term at minimum-scale amounts.

## Hard-cap / apron interactions (noted; full treatment in aprons bucket)

- **Taxpayer MLE (EXC-013):** usable only above the First Apron Level; signing it has apron-side consequences.
- **Traded Player $250K allowance (EXC-017–021):** reduced to $0 when post-trade Apron Team Salary would exceed the First Apron Level.
- **§6(f)(5) conversion (EXC-013):** a Non-Taxpayer MLE signing can be deemed a Taxpayer MLE when a later transaction pushes the team over the First Apron Level.

## Cross-rule dependencies

- **§6(n) eligibility/accounting (EXC-028, EXC-029)** governs the Disabled Player, Bi-annual, Non-Taxpayer MLE, Taxpayer MLE, and Traded Player exceptions — except the room-based traded-player provisions §6(j)(1)(v) and §6(j)(2).
- **Non-Aggregation (EXC-027)** bars combining exceptions except within the §6(j) traded-player framework.
- **Room MLE (EXC-014)** is mutually exclusive in-year with the Bi-annual and both MLEs.
