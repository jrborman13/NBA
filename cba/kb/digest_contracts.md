# Contracts Rules Digest — 2023 NBA CBA

Structured rules file: `rules_contracts.json` (bucket: `contracts`, 43 rules, `CONTRACT-001`–`CONTRACT-043`).
All citations point to the **printed** page numbers. Dollar/scale figures in Exhibits B and C are CBA **baseline** values subject to annual escalation (flagged for review).

## Notes on scope decisions
- Compensation-protection categories (lack of skill, death, basketball injury, illness, mental disability) in Art II §4(a)-(e) are termination/protection mechanics rather than discrete max/min/length/option/extension contract-structure rules; only the death cap and life-insurance limits (§4(j)) were extracted as a contracts rule. Waiver/stretch payment mechanics (§4(k), Art VII §7(d)(5)-(6)) and promotional-activity obligations (Art II §8) are out of the contracts bucket scope defined for this task.
