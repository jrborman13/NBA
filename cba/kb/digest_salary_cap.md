# Salary Cap Rules Digest — NBA 2023 CBA (Article VII §§2-4)

Bucket: `salary_cap`. Scope: how the Salary Cap, Minimum Team Salary, Tax Level, and Apron Levels are set/calculated; what counts as Salary; cap holds; incomplete-roster charges. Exceptions themselves and apron *transaction* restrictions live in other buckets.

Citations reference Article VII and **printed page** numbers.

---

## 1. Setting the Salary Cap & Tier Levels (§2(a))

- **CAP-001 — Salary Cap formula.** 44.74% of Projected BRI minus Projected Benefits, divided by team count (excl. first-two-year expansion teams). *VII §2(a)(1), p.169.*
- **CAP-002 — Expansion-adjusted cap override.** Alternative calc in a second-season expansion year if it produces a higher cap. *VII §2(a)(2), p.170.*
- **CAP-003 — Interim Audit true-up.** Interim-vs-final differences debited/credited (with T-Bill interest) to a later year. *VII §2(a)(3), pp.169-170.*
- **CAP-004 — Minimum Team Salary = 90% of cap.** *VII §2(a)(4)(i), p.170.*
- **CAP-005 — Tax Level = 121.5% of cap.** *VII §2(a)(4)(ii), pp.170,172.*
- **CAP-006 — First Apron Level.** Anchored to 2023-24 (Tax Level + scaled $6.716M); later years scale with the cap. *VII §2(a)(4)(iii)(A), pp.171-173.*
- **CAP-007 — Second Apron Level.** Anchored to 2023-24 (Tax Level + $17.5M); later years scale with the cap. *VII §2(a)(4)(iii)(B), pp.171-173.*
- **CAP-008 — Shortfall Amount increase.** With zero Carryover, next year's cap and all tiers rise by per-team Shortfall. *VII §2(a)(5)(i), p.173.*
- **CAP-009 — Overage Amount reduction.** Overage above 6% of Total Salaries & Benefits reduces next year's cap/tiers via a 6-step formula. *VII §2(a)(5)(ii), pp.173-175.*
- **CAP-010 — Year-over-year collar.** No cap/tier may fall below prior year or exceed 110% of it. *VII §2(a)(6), p.174.*
- **CAP-014 — Interim values fallback.** Interim/Estimated figures used when the Audit Report or BRI agreement is unavailable. *VII §2(a)(7), pp.175-176.*
- **CAP-015 — Effective period.** Cap/tiers run first day through last day of the Salary Cap Year. *VII §2(a)(8), p.176.*

## 2. Operating the Cap (§2(b))

- **CAP-016 — Basic rule.** Team Salary may not exceed the cap except via Exceptions. *VII §2(b)(1), p.176.*
- **CAP-017 — Room.** Team with Room may sign a contract whose first-year salary ≤ current Room. *VII §2(b)(2), p.176.*

## 3. Minimum Team Salary Operation (§2(c))

- **CAP-018 — Below-minimum payment & tax-share forfeiture.** Pay the NBA the shortfall; lose tax-distribution share. *VII §2(c)(1)-(2), pp.176-178.*
- **CAP-019 — In-season MTS cap hold.** Team Salary grossed up by any MTS shortfall; same-day-plus-one cure if it drops below MTS Threshold. *VII §2(c)(3)-(4), pp.177-178.*
- **CAP-020 — Year-end MTS true-up.** Remaining gap paid; due within 10 business days of Governing Audit Report; redistributed equally. *VII §2(c)(5)-(6), pp.178-179.*
- **CAP-021 — 2023-24 carve-out.** Below-minimum non-taxpayer still gets a 50% tax share that year only. *VII §2(c)(7), pp.178-179.*
- **CAP-022 — Above-minimum allowed.** Team Salary may exceed the minimum but not the cap (plus Exceptions). *VII §2(c)(8), pp.178-179.*

## 4. Tax Level Operation (§2(d))

- **CAP-011 — Tax Bracket Amount.** $5M scaled by current cap ÷ 2023-24 cap. *VII §2(d)(1)(ii), pp.181-182.*
- **CAP-012 — Luxury tax (Standard & Repeater).** Incremental tax above Tax Level; Repeater rates if over the line in 3+ of prior 4 years. *VII §2(d)(2), pp.182-185.*
- **CAP-013 — Tax Team Salary definition.** Team Salary at last regular-season game with incentive/suspension/min-salary adjustments. *VII §2(d)(1)(i), pp.179-181.*
- **CAP-023 — Tax revenue use.** NBA property; up to 50% to non-taxpayers, rest to League purposes. *VII §2(d)(3)-(4), pp.185-186.*

## 5. Apron Levels & Second-Apron Penalty (§2(e)-(f))

- **CAP-024 — Apron Team Salary definition.** Team Salary plus/minus a specified list (Performance Bonuses, free-agent holds, RFA offers, tender, deemed exceptions, incomplete-roster hold). *VII §2(e)(1), pp.186-187.* (Transaction restrictions are in the apron bucket.)
- **CAP-025 — Second Apron Team & Draft Pick Penalty.** Over Second Apron at last reg-season game → frozen pick; 2+ of next 4 years over → pick slides to final first-round slot. *VII §2(f), pp.195-197.*

## 6. Expansion Teams (§2(g))

- **CAP-026 — First-year cap = 66-2/3% of normal cap** (minimum 90% of that). *VII §2(g)(1), p.197.*
- **CAP-027 — Second-year cap = 80% of normal cap** (minimum 90% of that). *VII §2(g)(2), pp.197-198.*

## 7. Determination of Salary (§3)

- **CAP-028 — Deferred Compensation** counts in the year earned. *VII §3(a)(1), p.198.*
- **CAP-029 — Over 38 Rule** reallocates late-contract salary pro rata to earlier years. *VII §3(a)(2), pp.198-200.*
- **CAP-030 — Signing bonuses** defined (incl. trade & excess-international) and prorated by protected base compensation. *VII §3(b)(1)-(2), pp.200-201.*
- **CAP-031 — Incentive Compensation.** Likely bonuses count, Unlikely excluded; Expert challenge mechanism. *VII §3(d), pp.206-208.*
- **CAP-032 — International player payments** above the Excluded amount become Salary; amount rises $825K→$975K. *VII §3(e), pp.208-210.*
- **CAP-033 — Salary exclusions bundle** ⚠️ — loans, one-year minimums, insurance reimbursement, averaging, conduct reductions, existing contracts. *VII §3(c),(f)-(j), pp.205-206,210-211.* **[review_flag]**

## 8. Determination of Team Salary & Cap Holds (§4)

- **CAP-034 — Team Salary inclusions.** Active/former salaries, waived/retired/grievance amounts, free-agent holds, offer sheets, draft & incomplete-roster holds, deemed exceptions. *VII §4(a), pp.211-217.*
- **CAP-035 — Free Agent Amounts.** Cap-hold percentages by free-agent type (Qualifying 150/190%, Early 130%, Non-Qualifying 120%, etc.). *VII §4(d), pp.217-219.*
- **CAP-036 — First Round Pick cap hold** = 120% of Rookie Scale Amount. *VII §4(e), pp.217-220.*
- **CAP-037 — Incomplete roster charge.** (12 − counted players) × zero-YOS minimum salary, July 1 to day before season. *VII §4(f), pp.220-221.*
- **CAP-038 — Renouncing a Veteran Free Agent.** Removes the Free Agent Amount; limits re-signing; narrow rescission right; cannot renounce an RFA. *VII §4(g), pp.221-222.*
- **CAP-039 — Long-term (career-ending) injury exclusion.** Excluded after waiting period; 25-game return re-inclusion. *VII §4(h), pp.222-224.*
- **CAP-040 — Team Salary exclusions bundle** ⚠️ — assigned-contract timing, expansion waivers, two-way, summer, Exhibit 10, second-round-pick exception. *VII §4(b),(c),(i)-(l), pp.211-212,224-225.* **[review_flag]**
