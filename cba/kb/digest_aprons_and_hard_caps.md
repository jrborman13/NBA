# Digest — Aprons & Hard Caps (2023 NBA CBA, Article VII)

Bucket: `aprons_and_hard_caps`. Scope: which transactions each apron restricts/forbids, and what triggers a hard cap. Source sections: VII §2 (cap/apron levels, transaction restrictions table, draft-pick penalty), VII §6 (exceptions), VII §8 (trades). Citations below reference **printed** page numbers.

## How the levels are set
- **APRON-001 — First Apron Level** (p.171): 2023-24 = Tax Level + $6.716M (fixed base, scaled by a cap-growth fraction); later years scale by year cap ÷ 2023-24 cap.
- **APRON-002 — Second Apron Level** (p.171): 2023-24 = Tax Level + $17.5M (fixed base); later years scale by year cap ÷ 2023-24 cap.
- **APRON-003 — Apron Team Salary** (p.186-187): the adjusted salary figure (Team Salary ± a defined list of add-backs/subtractions) that every apron test measures against — not raw Team Salary.

## Core mechanic
- **APRON-004 — Transaction Restrictions Table engine** (p.187): for each listed transaction, (A) you can't do it if Apron Team Salary would then exceed the table's Applicable Apron Level, and (B) once you do it you're **hard-capped** at that apron for the rest of the year.
- **APRON-005 — Offseason look-ahead for rows E-J** (p.188-189): post-Regular-Season, rows E-J are also tested against next year's apron under the §2(e)(3) projection assumptions, extending the hard cap into the following season.

|---|---|---|

| APRON-008 | C | Acquire a Section 8(e)(1) sign-and-trade player |
| APRON-009 | D | In-season signing of a terminated player who had been earning above the Non-Taxpayer MLE (buyout-market limit) |
| APRON-010 | E | Expanded Traded Player Exception |
| APRON-011 | F | Standard TPE used after the season in which it arose (carried-over / prior-year TPE) |
| APRON-012 | G | Transition Traded Player Exception (2023-24 only) |

| Rule | Table row | Transaction |
|---|---|---|
| APRON-013 | H | Aggregated Standard TPE — i.e., **aggregating salaries** in a trade |
| APRON-014 | I | **Paying cash** to another team in a trade |

| APRON-016 | K | Taxpayer Mid-Level Salary Exception signing |

All table-row pages: p.190-191.

## Take-back / trade tightening tied to the aprons
- **APRON-017 — Expanded TPE take-back lost above first apron** (p.241, 190): above the first apron, teams fall back to the narrower Standard TPE matching room.
- **APRON-025 — $250K TPE buffer → $0 above first apron** (p.242): the small extra incoming-salary cushion disappears once post-trade Apron Team Salary would exceed the first apron.
- **APRON-019 — General cash-in-trade cap** (p.260): all teams limited to 5.15% of the Salary Cap in cash per year (paid + received not netted); the second-apron row-I rule (APRON-014) sits on top, banning any outgoing cash.

## Sign-and-trade specifics
- **APRON-018 — Section 8(e)(1) sign-and-trade conditions** (p.262-263): 3-4 year contract, not via Non-Taxpayer/Room MLE, first year fully protected, signed before the Regular Season, acquiring team must have **Room**; expressly subject to §2(e), which is why row C hard-caps the acquirer at the first apron.

## Taxpayer MLE prior-use lockout
- **APRON-020 — Taxpayer MLE prior use blocks later transactions same year** (p.188-189): after signing with the Taxpayer MLE, a team can't do rows A-E (2023-24) / rows A-F (2024-25 on) for the rest of that year.

## Second-apron draft-pick penalties
- **APRON-022 — Second Apron Team definition** (p.195): Apron Team Salary exceeds the Second Apron Level as of the team's last Regular Season game.
- **APRON-023 — Frozen first-rounder** (p.195-196): a Second Apron Team (2024-25 on) can't trade its first-round pick seven seasons out.
- **APRON-024 — Draft Pick Penalty** (p.196-197): if a Second Apron Team in ≥2 of the next four years, that frozen pick drops to the last selection of the first round; if in <2 of four years, the pick is freed (no penalty).

## Transitional relief
- **APRON-021 — 2023-24 carve-out for rows F-J** (p.191): in 2023-24 only, those transactions don't by themselves bar exceeding the applicable apron that year (the remainder-of-year hard cap from those rows is suspended for 2023-24).

## Notes on dollar bases
- Fixed dollar bases: $6.716M (first apron add), $17.5M (second apron add), $250K TPE buffer, $5M Taxpayer MLE base (then cap-scaled).
- Percentage bases: 5.15% of cap (cash-in-trade), 9.12% of cap (Non-Taxpayer MLE), 3.32% of cap (Bi-annual), and apron-level scaling fractions (year cap ÷ 2023-24 cap).
