## Articles judged NOT relevant to transaction-checking (please confirm)

These articles do not contain transaction-eligibility/compliance rules and were **not** mined for rule objects:

- **III** Player Expenses; **IV** Benefits; **V** Military Duty Compensation — player welfare/benefits, not roster transactions.
- **VI** Player Conduct; **XXXIII** Anti-Drug Program — discipline/health; a suspension can affect eligibility but the transaction rules that matter are captured where Article VII/XI reference them.
- **XV** Certifications; **XVI** Mutual Reservation of Rights; **XVII** Playing Conditions; **XVIII** Travel/Locker Room; **XIX** Union Security; **XX** Scheduling; **XXI** All-Star Game; **XXII** Player Health & Wellness; **XXIII** Exhibition/Off-Season Games; **XXV** Deferred Compensation Limitation*; **XXVI** Team Rules; **XXVII** Right of Set-Off; **XXVIII** Media Rights; **XXIX** Miscellaneous; **XXX** No-Strike/No-Lockout; **XXXI** Grievance & Arbitration; **XXXII** System Arbitration; **XXXIV** Recognition; **XXXV** Savings; **XXXVI** Player Agents; **XXXVII** Player Appearances; **XXXVIII** Integration/Interpretation; **XXXIX** Term; **XL** Expansion/Contraction; **XLII** Other.

\* **Article XXV (Deferred Compensation)** and **Article XIII (Circumvention)** are borderline — they constrain how contracts may be structured. Article XIII (Circumvention) IS mined where it bears on contract/trade legality. Article XXV is noted but treated as a structural cap-accounting rule already covered by Article VII §5; flag for confirmation if you want it broken out separately.

**Borderline / partially mined:** Article X (Player Eligibility & NBA Draft) is mined only for draft-rights and signing-eligibility items that gate transactions (e.g., draft-pick rights, two-way eligibility), not for draft-process mechanics.
