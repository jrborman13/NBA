# Free Agency — Rules Digest

Source: 2023 NBA CBA, **Article XI (Free Agency)** with cross-references to **Article VII, Section 6(b)** (Veteran Free Agent re-signing exceptions: Bird / Early Bird / Non-Bird).
Rules file: `rules_free_agency.json` (39 rules, prefix `FA-`). Page citations are to **printed page numbers**.

---

## 1. Free Agent Types

| Type | How determined | Key rules |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| **Unrestricted Free Agent (UFA)** | No Qualifying Offer made (or ROFR relinquished / QO withdrawn). Free to sign with any Team. | FA-001 |
| **Restricted Free Agent (RFA)** | Prior Team makes a timely Qualifying Offer to an eligible player → subject to Right of First Refusal. | FA-002, FA-007, FA-016, FA-018 |

A player who withholds services for **more than 30 days** after the start of his contract's last Season is **not** a Veteran Free Agent and cannot sign elsewhere without his last Team's consent (FA-015).

## 2. Free Agency Timing (Article XI §1(a))

- **6:00 p.m. ET, June 30** — negotiations open (UFAs with any Team; RFAs with Prior Team + Offer Sheets with other Teams). FA-001, FA-002
- **12:01 p.m. ET, first day of Moratorium Period** — RFAs may **sign Offer Sheets** with non-Prior Teams. FA-002
- **After Moratorium Period ends** — UFAs may sign with any Team; RFAs may sign with Prior Team. FA-001, FA-002
- **Early-negotiation carve-outs** (negotiation only, not signing): roster players the day after the Season (FA-003); Non-Draft Rookies right after their Draft (FA-004).
- No public statement of agreement before Moratorium ends (FA-006; 2023-24 player exemption). No public "we'll match" / specific-offer statements about RFAs (FA-013).
- Timing violations: Commissioner may fine up to **$2,000,000**, forfeit picks, suspend personnel (FA-005).

## 3. Qualifying Offers (QO) — Article XI §1(e), §4

**Form (FA-008):** one-year Uniform Player Contract, Team-signed, 100% Base Compensation protection (lack of skill + injury/illness), no individually-negotiated conditions.

**Amounts by player category:**
- **First Round Picks finishing Rookie Scale** (FA-009): 4th-year salary × Rookie-Scale QO% column; **9th-player amount** for picks 10+ meeting **Starter Criteria**; **15th-player amount** (lesser-of test) for picks 1-14 failing Starter Criteria.
- **Two-Way Contract finishers** (FA-010): Standard NBA Contract at Minimum Player Salary (multi-year/repeat Two-Way) with **$90,000** Standard/Two-Way QO Protection (2023-24, scaled thereafter), or a Two-Way Qualifying Offer.
- **All other ROFR players** (FA-011): **135%** of last-year salary (**125%** if prior contract pre-2023-24); **Minimum-Plus floor** = Minimum Annual Salary + **$200,000**; 21st-player amount for qualifying 2nd-round/undrafted players.
- Other terms must match prior contract; optional Exhibit 6 physical (FA-012).

**What makes a player Restricted:**
- First Round Picks: QO window = day after 2nd Option Year Season → **5:00 p.m. ET June 29**. QO → RFA; no QO → UFA (FA-016).
- Other Veteran Free Agents with **≤ 3 Years of Service** or completing a Two-Way Contract: same June 29 window (FA-018).

**Maximum Qualifying Offer** (FA-017): optional, only with a QO to a first-round pick — 5 Seasons, first year at Maximum Annual Salary, 8% annual raises, full protection, no Option/ETO. Triggers the longer-Offer-Sheet rule (FA-022).

**QO acceptance / withdrawal deadlines (FA-019):**
- Accept by **October 1** (extendable, unlimited, never beyond **March 1**).
- Team may withdraw freely through **July 13**; on/after **July 14** withdrawal also **renounces** the player.
- Withdrawal → player immediately becomes a UFA.

**Validity challenges (FA-020):** notice within **10 days**; Team may cure within **5 business days**.

**Reasserting ROFR in later years (FA-021):** if no Offer Sheet signed by **March 1** and no re-signing that Season, Team reasserts by re-tendering an identical QO by **5:00 p.m. ET June 29**.

## 4. Offer Sheets & Right of First Refusal — Article XI §5

- **Offer Sheet (FA-022):** RFA + New Team sign; given to ROFR Team. Term **> 1 Season** (**> 2 Seasons** if both QO and Max QO tendered); not Two-Way; New Team must hold **Room** throughout.
- **Matching means (FA-023):** ROFR Team may match using **Room**, a **Veteran Free Agent Exception** (Art. VII §6(b)), or the **Minimum Player Salary Exception**; must hold ≥ first-year Salary + Unlikely Bonuses continuously.
- **Gilbert Arenas rule (FA-024):** RFA with **1-2 Years of Service** — first-year cap = Non-Taxpayer Mid-Level amount; 3rd-year balloon; 4th-year change ≤ 4.5%; Room computed on **averaged** salary.
- **Principal Terms (FA-025):** term, fixed/specified compensation, qualifying Incentive Compensation (Likely Bonuses tied to the two Teams + Generally Recognized League Honors), allowable UPC amendments.

### The 48-hour / matching window (FA-026)
- Offer Sheet received **before 12:00 p.m. ET** → match by **11:59 p.m. ET next day**.
- Offer Sheet received **at/after 12:00 p.m. ET** → match by **11:59 p.m. ET two days later**.
- Match → player bound to ROFR Team on Principal Terms; **no amendment for 1 year**.
- **No match / decline (FA-027):** player + New Team bound to full attached contract; no amendment for 1 year.
- **Moratorium extension (FA-028):** Offer Sheets received during Moratorium → match deadline **11:59 p.m. ET July 7** after the Moratorium.

### Physicals
- **New Team Exhibit 6 (FA-031):** physical within **2 days** of execution, results reported within **3 days**; failure to notify = deemed passed.
- **ROFR conditional match (FA-030):** ROFR Team may condition its Notice on a physical within 2 days; detailed non-submission / failure / withdrawal rules.
- **Failed-physical fallout (FA-032):** ROFR Team must elect within **2 days** to keep prior rights or relinquish.

### Other ROFR mechanics
- Post-match: **no trade for 1 year** without consent; never to the matched Team for 1 year (FA-029).
- No exercising ROFR pursuant to a pre-arranged sign-and-trade (FA-033, Art. VII §8(e)).
- Only **one** outstanding Offer Sheet; revoke only by unanimous written consent (FA-034).
- Relinquishing ROFR (FA-035): allowed except during a pending QO acceptance period; makes player a UFA + renounces him.
- Disputes → expedited **System Arbitrator**, resolved within **5 days** (FA-036).
- **March 1** is the last day an RFA may give an Offer Sheet; no inter-Team consideration over ROFR/Offer Sheet decisions (FA-037).
- Notice/delivery rules; Teams designate 3 email reps by **June 10** (FA-038).

## 5. Rights Tiers — Bird / Early Bird / Non-Bird (Article VII §6(b), cross-ref) — FA-039

Re-signing power above the Salary Cap with the **Prior Team**, beginning 12:01 p.m. ET on the last day of the Moratorium Period:

| Tier (common name) | Max first-year salary | Term requirement |
|--------------------|-----------------------|------------------|
| **Qualifying Veteran Free Agent (Bird)** | Up to the maximum (Art. II §7) | — |
| **Early Qualifying Veteran Free Agent (Early Bird)** | Greater of 175% of prior Regular Salary or 105% of prior-year Average Player Salary | **≥ 2 Seasons** |
| **Non-Qualifying Veteran Free Agent (Non-Bird)** | Greater of 120% prior Regular Salary, 120% Minimum Annual Salary, or (own RFA) the QO amount | — |

> **Note:** Full *qualification* mechanics for each tier (how many Years of Service with the Prior Team, continuity-of-service rules) live in the Article I definitions of Qualifying / Early Qualifying / Non-Qualifying Veteran Free Agent, which are **not** in the provided source excerpts. FA-039 captures the re-signing power; tier qualification should be reviewed against those definitions.
